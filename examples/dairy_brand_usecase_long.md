## English Script (Long Version - 2-4min)

**How does a trusted national dairy brand reclaim the hearts of Gen Z?**

Brand A, a leading dairy company, faced a brutal truth【On-screen: "Trusted ≠ Desired"】: young consumers saw them as reliable, but never exciting. The brand was "fridge insurance," not social currency.

The team opened Atypica【Recording 1: Open product interface, 3-5 seconds】and entered their core question【Recording 2: Input "How do we rebuild brand relevance with Gen Z?" in dialog box, 5-8 seconds】.

Traditional brand audits couldn't reveal *why* Gen Z turned away, or *what to do next*.

To provide context, they uploaded three benchmark studies【Recording 3: Drag files to upload area - showing "Heinz_brand_study.pdf", "Nike_Gen_Z_strategy.pdf", "Coca_Cola_AI_campaign.pdf", 8-10 seconds】.

Heinz, after 150 years, became social currency by handing users the pen — the "Draw Ketchup" experiment turned recognition into participatory proof. Nike stopped preaching victory and started validating effort, shifting from "Just Do It" to "Why Do It?" Coca-Cola made scarcity the strategy, launching AI co-created mystery flavors as collectible drops.

Three patterns emerged【On-screen: 3 keywords】: **symbol over statement**, **sovereignty handoff**, and **scarcity as currency**.

These became the lens for what came next.

Atypica sourced five personas【Recording 4: Persona cards generate sequentially - Lin, Qiang, Zhou, Mochi, Vivian, 10-15 seconds】from cross-platform behavioral data.

Each sat for deep interviews【Recording 5: Switch to dialog interface, showing Moderator asking "Would you share a brand if it let you design the product?" → Lin responding "I'd share it if it felt like my choice, not my mom's.", 15-20 seconds】— but not generic questions. Each was tested against the patterns.

Zhou, the coder from City B【Quote card】: *"If they co-created with users, I'd believe it's real, not just marketing."*

The research surfaced two core problems — and one strategic path forward【Recording 6: Switch to report page, scroll to "Core Findings" section, 20-25 seconds】.

**First, the perception gap【Recording 6 cont.: Highlight "Perception Gap" paragraph】.** Brand A was trusted, but not desired. Users bought it out of habit, not identity. The brand had reliability equity, but zero aspirational pull.

**Second, the execution trap【Recording 6 cont.: Highlight "Execution Trap" paragraph】.** Marketing spoke *at* Gen Z with celebrity endorsements — not *with* them. As one persona put it【Quote card】: *"It feels like my dad trying to use internet slang."*

**Third, the solution blueprint【Recording 6 cont.: Scroll to "Strategic Recommendations", sequentially highlight 5 levers】.** Five actionable levers emerged:

→ Create a flagship moment that redefines perception  
→ Unify visual identity across all sub-brands  
→ Pivot from product claims to lifestyle identity  
→ Enable co-creation — packaging, flavors, ownership  
→ Filter partnerships by cultural DNA, not hype

atypica delivered the full roadmap【Recording 7: Switch to report table of contents page, showing complete chapter structure - Executive Summary, Core Findings, Strategic Recommendations, Implementation Roadmap, 10-15 seconds】in two weeks【On-screen: "6 months → 2 weeks"】.

**From diagnosis to direction. Real benchmarks. Real action.**
