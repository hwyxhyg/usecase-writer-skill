## English Script (Short Version - 1-2min)

**How does a trusted national dairy brand reclaim the hearts of Gen Z?**

Brand A, a leading dairy company, faced a brutal truth【On-screen: "Trusted ≠ Desired"】: young consumers saw them as reliable, but never exciting. The brand was "fridge insurance," not social currency.

Their question was direct【Product UI: Question input to dialog box - "How do we rebuild brand relevance with Gen Z?"】

Traditional brand audits couldn't reveal *why* Gen Z turned away, or *what to do next*.

atypica started with global benchmarks【Visual: Heinz + Nike + Coca-Cola logos expand】.

Heinz became social currency by handing users the pen. Nike stopped preaching victory, started validating effort. Coca-Cola made scarcity the strategy with AI mystery flavors.

Three patterns emerged【On-screen: 3 keywords display sequentially】: **symbol over statement**, **sovereignty handoff**, and **scarcity as currency**.

atypica sourced five personas【Visual: 5 Persona cards animation】and tested each against the patterns.

Lin, a professional from City A【Quote card】: *"I'd share it if it felt like my choice, not my mom's."*

Zhou, a coder from City B【Quote card】: *"If they co-created with users, I'd believe it's real."*

The research surfaced two core problems【Visual: Chart animation】.

**First, the perception gap.** Trusted, but not desired.

**Second, the execution trap.** Marketing spoke *at* Gen Z, not *with* them.

**Third, the solution blueprint【Product UI: Report overview - showing table of contents】.** Five actionable levers emerged:

→ Create a flagship moment  
→ Unify visual identity  
→ Pivot narrative  
→ Enable co-creation  
→ Filter partnerships

atypica delivered in two weeks【On-screen: "6 months → 2 weeks"】.

**From diagnosis to direction.**
